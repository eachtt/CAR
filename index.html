TL;DR

CAR improves a frozen VLA policy at test time. A small critic and an action refiner, trained on world-model rollouts scored by a VLM verifier, pick and refine the policy's action chunks.

How it works

Method

CAR has three offline steps and one deployment step. The frozen VLA policy is never updated.

01

Imagine

offline

At each demonstration state, a fine-tuned world model rolls out the candidate action chunks.

  • Candidates are the ground-truth action chunk a*, K samples from the frozen VLA policy, and perturbations of a* with known offsets.
  • The world model is fine-tuned on the robot's own demonstrations.
02

Judge

offline

A point tracker converts each imagined future into motion facts. A general VLM verifier labels each candidate with a score from 0 to 100 and a correction.

  • Its in-context memory holds the rollout of the demonstration and the rollouts of the known perturbations.
  • The VLM is not fine-tuned.
03

Critic and Refine

offline

The labels supervise the critic. The action refiner is trained on the critic's scores.

  • The critic predicts the score and the correction of an action chunk.
  • The action refiner moves an action chunk toward a better-scored candidate of the same state.
04

Deployment

run time

The frozen VLA policy samples K action chunks. The critic picks the best one, and the action refiner moves it toward the goal score s*.

  • Neither the world model nor the VLM verifier runs at deployment.
  • The two networks add 15 ms per chunk on one RTX A6000.
